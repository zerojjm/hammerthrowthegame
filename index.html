How to play

Getting started

Press START. Touch near the 0° START marker and trace the green handle guide. Your finger controls the handle; the hammer ball follows under its own inertia. Make two preliminary winds, then three or four full turns. Lift your finger to release.

Build speed smoothly

Accelerate gradually and follow the moving handle guide. During turns, the athlete advances through the throwing circle. Staying close to the optimal guide radius helps preserve a long hammer orbit. Moving too far outside the guide causes a foul.

Both throwing directions

Start at the bottom and rotate either counterclockwise (right-handed) or clockwise (left-handed). The game detects the direction during the first wind and mirrors single support, uphill/downhill timing, and the small sector allowance automatically. Keep rotating in the chosen direction.

Double support (DS): drive the hammer

The easiest time to accelerate is immediately after single support ends, on the descending side, until the low point at 0°. You can continue adding speed after the low point, until the next single-support phase begins, but uphill acceleration becomes more demanding.

Single support (SS): match the ball

During SS, the athlete has very little ability to add speed. Make your finger follow the hammer's existing pace instead of forcing it ahead. The ball naturally loses some speed as it travels uphill. Extended, excessive hammer lag during turns causes a loss-of-balance foul; momentary lag is allowed. When DS resumes, accelerate smoothly again.

Why radius matters

If you accelerate the handle faster than the ball can follow, the ball lags and its effective orbit radius can shrink. This costs speed, especially at high speeds. Recovering a shortened orbit back to a larger radius can cost additional speed. The goal is to keep the ball radius as large and stable as possible throughout the throw.

Release and results

Release after at least three complete turns (two winds plus three turns) by lifting your finger. The ball flies along the tangent to its actual orbit. The result shows distance and charts for ball speed and orbit radius; purple chart bands indicate single support.

Game mode

Targets: 35, 45, 55, 60, 65 and 75 m. One free retry after a foul per target; additional retries or retries after valid throws spend the shared bank of three. The best valid result for each target counts.
